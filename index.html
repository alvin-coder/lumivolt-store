Straight answers

Where do orders ship from?

Depending on the item, from supplier warehouses in the US, EU or Asia — always with a tracking number. US-warehouse items typically arrive in 3–7 business days; items shipping from Asia average 8–15 days. The estimate for each product is shown before you pay.

How do returns work?

30 days from delivery, for any reason, on unopened items in original condition. If something arrives damaged or dead-on-arrival, we replace or refund — and we chase the supplier, not you. Contact support with your order number to start.

How do I reach support?

Email support@lumivolt.example with your order number — we reply within 24 hours. Order issues (damaged, lost, dead-on-arrival) are handled first, ahead of general questions.

Are these products certified?

Electrical items ship with the certifications required for the destination market (FCC/CE and similar). If a product listing ever loses its certification status with a supplier, we pull it rather than sell through it.

What payment methods do you take?

Card via our payment provider, plus PayPal at checkout. Payment data never touches our servers — it goes direct to the processor (Stripe/PayPal) over an encrypted connection.
