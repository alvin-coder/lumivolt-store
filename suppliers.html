Deep dive: our shortlist

CJdropshipping

Primary pick · free forever

The strongest free all-rounder for this catalog: one-click imports, sourcing requests answered in 24–72h, US/EU warehouses for 3–7 day domestic delivery on stocked SKUs, plus POD and custom packaging when the brand grows.

⚠ Quality varies by SKU — order a sample of every product before listing it. Disputes under $5K monthly volume can take 48–72h.

Use for: EchoPods, LumaRing, AirScout, PixoBeam — anything where we need sourcing flexibility and a US warehouse option.

cjdropshipping.com →

Zendrop

Fast shipping · free plan

Best onboarding experience of any dropshipping platform — import a product in under five minutes. US-warehoused inventory means the shipping estimates we promise customers are estimates we can keep.

⚠ Deepest automation and branding tools require the paid tier — fine to start free and upgrade only when order volume justifies it.

Use for: any SKU where delivery speed drives the sale (SentryDot, Voltra Deck, TrackTag).

zendrop.com →

AliExpress + DSers

Testing layer · both free

Still the cheapest way to test demand on a new gadget before committing to a CJ or Zendrop line. DSers (free plan) batches orders and syncs tracking automatically.

⚠ Treat as a testing layer only — 15–45 day shipping and variable seller quality make it a poor permanent fulfillment base. Never list a product you haven't sampled.

Use for: validating new product ideas with small ad spend before moving winners to CJ/Zendrop lines.

dsers.com →

Spocket

US/EU focus · free plan

Curated US/EU suppliers with domestic 2–7 day delivery. Product costs run higher than China-sourced, but shipping speed and perceived quality lift conversion on higher-ticket items.

⚠ Catalog is smaller and per-item costs are higher — reserve it for hero SKUs where fast delivery justifies the margin hit.

Use for: LumaRing and MemoKey — premium items where "ships from the US" is a selling point.

spocket.co →

Printful / Printify

Print-on-demand · pay per item

For the moment Lumivolt adds branded merch (tees, totes, device pouches): both are free to start, print per order, and integrate with the store in minutes.

⚠ Per-unit cost is high at low volume — price merch at 2.5–3x landed cost minimum, and design for the product (dark prints on light garments, etc.).

Use for: future brand merch line — not the current gadget catalog.

printful.com →

Syncee

Verified marketplace · free plan

A curated marketplace where suppliers are verified before listing — the cleanest sourcing funnel if we expand beyond gadgets into curated home goods.

⚠ The free plan caps product imports; paid tiers unlock the real catalog. Budget for it only when the store has steady sales.

Use for: category expansion — home & light products beyond the current ten.

syncee.co →

Why these, and not others

Three platforms commonly recommended elsewhere didn't make the list. Doba dropped its free tier entirely (paid monthly only). Wholesale2b and Importify are paid-only tools with no free path. Worldwide Brands and SaleHoo are legitimate but charge upfront/annual fees — useful later, wrong for a $0 launch.

Scam red flags — the checklist that keeps you safe

Compiled from FTC guidance, BC government consumer alerts, and 2026 operator reports. Any supplier exhibiting two or more of these — walk away.

  • Ongoing "membership" feesRecurring fees are rarely standard for real suppliers — you pay for products and sometimes a small order fee, nothing more.
  • No verifiable business addressIf they can't show a physical warehouse/office address, they may not hold the inventory they claim.
  • Wholesale prices without asking for business proofReal suppliers verify you're a reseller (EIN/business registration). "Sell to anyone at wholesale" is a red flag, not a feature.
  • Refuses product samplesSample refusal often signals replica goods or quality issues. Non-negotiable: we sample every SKU before listing.
  • Unbelievably low pricesIf a price is far below every other supplier, the "product" is often counterfeit, used, or simply never ships.
  • Payment only by wire/gift card/cryptoLegitimate suppliers take cards or protected B2B payment rails. Irreversible payment methods exist to defeat chargebacks — yours.
  • Vague "service" or "processing" feesFees without clear definitions in the contract are where margin quietly dies. Ask for the full fee schedule in writing.
  • Unreachable support before you payTest every channel — email, chat, phone — before your first order. Slow pre-sale support means absent post-sale support.
  • Stock levels that seem too stableInventory that never moves on a "bestseller" suggests fake listings. Cross-check the same item on AliExpress/1688.
The one rule that matters most: order a sample of every product, to your own address, before it goes live in the store. It costs a few dollars and it is the only test that catches bad quality, slow fulfillment, and phantom inventory in one shot.
